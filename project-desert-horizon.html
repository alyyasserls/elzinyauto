Lamborghini Huracán EVO Spyder

V10 · 5.2L · 640 HP · Spyder · 2024

The Huracán EVO Spyder is the open-air masterpiece of Lamborghini's V10 lineage. Cloaked in pearl white and powered by a naturally aspirated 5.2-liter V10 producing 640 horsepower, it combines Lamborghini's revolutionary LDVI all-wheel drive system with the exhilarating sensation of roofless freedom. Inside, hand-stitched blue Alcantara meets carbon fiber in a cockpit designed to be felt as much as driven.

Engine 5.2L V10 Naturally Aspirated
Power 640 HP / 600 Nm
0–100 km/h 3.1 seconds
Top Speed 202 mph (325 km/h)
Body Style Spyder (Open-Top Convertible)
Interior Blue Alcantara & Carbon Fiber
Brakes Lamborghini Carboceramic (Blue Caliper)
Exterior Pearl White / Bianco Canopus

Born Under the Open Sky

The Huracán EVO Spyder's retractable soft-top drops in just 17 seconds at speeds up to 50 km/h, transforming a track-bred supercar into an open-air sensory experience. Every aerodynamic line, from the front splitter to the integrated rear diffuser, channels airflow engineered to deliver maximum downforce at speed.

A Cockpit in Blue

Step inside and the Spyder wraps you in hand-stitched blue Alcantara and carbon fiber — every surface deliberately chosen. The 8.4" HMI touchscreen integrates Apple CarPlay, telemetry, and advanced LDVI controls into a cockpit that feels like a fighter jet with the sky for a roof.
