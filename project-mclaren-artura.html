McLaren Artura

V6 Twin-Turbo Hybrid · 671 HP · Coupe · 2024

The full force of McLaren.

A High-Performance Hybrid powertrain bringing electrified intensity to a lightweight supercar. 0–60 mph in 3.0 seconds, wrapped in a striking blue exterior. The Artura is the next generation of supercar, combining a twin-turbocharged V6 engine with an electric motor for instant torque and blistering acceleration. The interior features bespoke materials, ensuring a visceral driving experience unlike any other.

Engine 3.0L V6 Twin-Turbo Hybrid
Power 671 HP
0–60 mph 3.0 seconds
Top Speed 205 mph
Body Style Coupe
Interior Black with Yellow Details
Transmission 8-Speed Seamless Shift
Exterior Striking Blue

Pioneering Design

The Artura embodies McLaren's super-lightweight engineering philosophy. Every curve is sculpted for aerodynamic efficiency and cooling, creating an unmistakable, aggressive profile that shrinks around the driver.

Visceral Engagement

The interior is built around the driver, focusing pure functionality into an art form. The aggressive yet comfortable seating positions you perfectly for high-G maneuvers, surrounded by tactile materials that connect you to the machine.
