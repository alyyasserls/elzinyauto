Mercedes-AMG G63

V8 Biturbo · 4.0L · 577 HP · SUV · 2024

The legend, reimagined. A hand-built 4.0L V8 biturbo producing 577 HP wrapped in obsidian black — with a bespoke cognac and black quilted leather interior. 0–60 in 4.5 seconds. The Mercedes-AMG G63 is pure AMG brutality dressed in unparalleled luxury, maintaining its iconic boxy silhouette while offering state-of-the-art technology and performance.

Engine 4.0L V8 Biturbo
Power 577 HP @ 6,000 rpm
0–60 mph 4.5 seconds
Top Speed 137 mph (149 mph optional)
Body Style Luxury Off-Road SUV
Interior Bespoke Cognac & Black Leather
Transmission 9-Speed AMG SPEEDSHIFT TCT
Exterior Obsidian Black Metallic

Iconic Presence

The G-Class has always been an icon of absolute capability and unmatched presence. The G63 amplifies this legacy with its AMG-specific radiator grille, flared wheel arches, and striking side pipes. It commands respect on any road, combining rugged military heritage with modern luxury aesthetics.

Unmatched Versatility

Whether cruising through the city or conquering impossible terrain, the G63 delivers an unparalleled driving experience. The interior surrounds you in hand-crafted luxury, while the advanced AMG suspension ensures dynamic handling that belies its imposing size.
